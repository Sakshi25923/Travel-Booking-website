Tarkarli Beach

Tarkarli Beach, in Maharashtra's Sindhudurg district, is a serene coastal spot famous for its pristine white sands, clear turquoise waters, and abundant water sports like scuba diving, snorkeling, and dolphin watching

Ganpatipule

Ganpatipule is best known as a destination for its temple-beach confluence, offering a blend of pilgrimage, tranquil relaxation, and scenic coastal beauty.

Sindhudurg Fort

Sindhudurg refers to both a historic sea fort and a district in Maharashtra's Konkan region, known for its beautiful coastline (Malvan, Tarkarli), pristine beaches, waterfalls, rich culture, and historic maritime heritage
